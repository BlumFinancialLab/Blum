# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to a professional, inclusive and evidence-focused community. Debate
technical and financial claims rigorously while treating contributors with
respect.

## Expected behavior

- Be precise, constructive and transparent about uncertainty.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and data limitations.
- Respect privacy, licensing and responsible disclosure.
- Do not promote harassment, discrimination, market manipulation or deceptive
  performance claims.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, reject or remove contributions and participation that
violate these standards. Serious security or safety concerns should be reported
privately as described in `SECURITY.md`.
